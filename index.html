Who is Sciar Tips for?

For restaurant owners who want a clear, consistent way to manage card-tip payouts, and for team members who want to understand their own payout.

⌁

Bring card tips together

Sciar Tips connects to a restaurant's card terminal and brings the card tips into a weekly pool.

⚙

Set the split rule

The restaurant owner sets the tip-splitting rule in the owner dashboard.

◎

Make personal payouts clear

Each staff member can view their own payout details in the staff view. They also receive a text with their own payout each Monday.

Product information is based on the supplied pilot-pack README.md ("The company") and the staff-view alpha built for this assignment.
