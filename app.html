7. Help and frequently asked questions

How are my tips calculated?

Weekly pool × your points ÷ everyone’s points that week. The result is rounded to cents.

How do I get points?

Your role has a points rate. Multiply that rate by your hours before 23:00.

Do late hours count in my total hours?

Yes. Late hours are already included in total hours. They are shown separately because they do not earn points under this pilot rule.

Why do hours after 23:00 earn no points?

That is the rule set for Halloran’s pilot. This page explains the rule; it does not decide whether the rule is fair.

Is the payment status real?

No. The paid/pending labels and dates are fictional examples for this prototype. The pilot files do not contain bank payment confirmation.

Who can see my concern?

In this demo, it stays in this browser. A real service needs a clear, secure concern-handling process.
